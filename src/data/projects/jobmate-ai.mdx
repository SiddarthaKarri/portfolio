# JobMate AI

An intelligent job application assistant that helps you land your dream job by optimizing your resume and generating tailored cover letters using Google's Gemini AI.

## Features

- **Resume Parsing**: Automatically extract skills, experience, and education from your PDF resume.
- **AI Cover Letters**: Generate professional, tailored cover letters based on your resume and the job description.
- **Job Tracking**: (Planned) Keep track of your applications and their status.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js 14, React, Tailwind CSS
- **AI**: Google Gemini Pro API
- **Deployment**: Vercel

## Challenges

Integrating the PDF parser to accurately extract structured data was a key challenge. Different resume formats required robust error handling and flexible prompts for the AI model to interpret the text correctly.
